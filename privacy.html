Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Snowy Blizzard Clips is a personal tool. Its only user is the owner of the Snowy Blizzard Clips accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

What it uses

  • Sign-in tokens for those accounts, so it can upload videos and read their view counts.
  • Basic account details (account name and id) to show which account is signed in.
  • The videos it uploads, with their titles and descriptions.
  • View counts of the videos it posted.

Where it's kept

Everything is stored only on the owner's own PC. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the platform it belongs to (YouTube, TikTok or Instagram). No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing it

Access can be removed at any time in each platform's account settings, under connected apps. Questions: snowyblizzardclips@gmail.com
